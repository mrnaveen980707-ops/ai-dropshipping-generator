Research Toolkit

Turn a product idea into a structured research brief for India or global markets. Use it to organize thinking—not as a substitute for real demand, supplier, cost, compliance or ad validation.

The output is generated from transparent rule-based templates in this page. It does not call an AI model or external data source.

Research brief
Validate before launch
  • Check search interest, marketplace demand and competitor saturation.
  • Confirm supplier reliability, landed cost, shipping time and return handling.
  • For India, model COD refusal / RTO risk before scaling.
  • Verify product safety, claims, IP/trademark rights and platform policies.
  • Test creatives and landing-page conversion with small controlled budgets.
